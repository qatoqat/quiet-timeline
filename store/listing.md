## Description

Quiet Timeline makes X (formerly Twitter) calm again. It turns down the noise and leaves the people you follow alone.

**Filter the noise**
• Bots and engagement farmers get a score from account age, posting rate, follower ratio, copy-pasted replies, bait phrases and more. You choose what happens at each level: dim, collapse or hide.
• Paid checkmarks no longer buy trust: dim or collapse their replies, and grey out or hide the badge.
• Slurs and profanity are masked or collapsed, including leetspeak and stretched spellings. You can add your own muted words.
• Repost, aggregator and single-topic accounts get a tag, and their media can be blurred, left unloaded until you click, or hidden. Their videos won't autoplay.
• Collapsed replies keep a single tidy line (name, handle, date, views) with a show/hide link.
• Optionally blur the avatars of accounts you don't follow, and media in replies.
• Promoted posts are hidden, and Premium, Grok and verification upsells and nag popups are closed.

**A quieter look**
• Soft low-contrast themes (Quiet, Graphite, Nord, Gruvbox, Mocha, OLED, Paper, or your own colors), calmer buttons and softer text. X Chat is themed too.
• Declutter: icon-only menu, hide the logo, no center borders, rounded corners, view counts next to the date, outlined checkmarks, and a quick "Not interested" button.

**Private by design**
Everything runs in your browser. No accounts, no analytics, no servers: Quiet Timeline makes no network requests of its own.

Not affiliated with X Corp.
